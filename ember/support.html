Restoring Ember Pro on a new device

Ember Pro is a one-time purchase tied to your Apple Account. On the new device, open Settings inside Ember and tap Restore Purchase, or tap Restore Purchase on the Ember Pro screen. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple Account you used to buy it.

Seven Nights to a Quieter Mind

Night 1 is free for everyone. Nights 2 through 7 are included with Ember Pro. Each night downloads the first time you play it, so you need an internet connection for that first play; after that it is saved on your device and plays offline.

Apple Health is not recording sessions

In Ember, open Settings and turn on Save to Apple Health. If it is already on, open the Health app → your profile → Apps → Ember, and confirm that Mindful Minutes is allowed. Ember only writes completed sessions; it never reads your Health data.

Daily reminders are not arriving

Open the iPhone Settings app → Notifications → Ember, and make sure notifications are allowed. Then check the reminder time inside Ember’s Settings.
